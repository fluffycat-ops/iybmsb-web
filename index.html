The studioSmall team, big rooms.

IYBMSB LLC is an independent mobile game studio. We make social games built for the same room — quick to start, easy to learn, and hard to put down. The best nights don't come with instructions, so neither do our games.

We build small and ship often. Every title starts from one question: what gets a room laughing, arguing, and asking for a rematch? Ghant and Bruk It Up are our first two answers — and there's more on the way.

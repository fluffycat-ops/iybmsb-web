The studioSmall studio, made for the same room.

IYBMSB LLC is a small independent studio building mobile games for people in the same place at the same time. We care about games that need no manual and start in seconds.

We build small and ship often. Every title begins with one question: what gets a room laughing, arguing, and asking for a rematch? Ghant and Bruk It Up are our first two answers — with more in development.
